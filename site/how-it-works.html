Where it answers

It answers where you work. With the same receipts.

Ask in Slack or Microsoft Teams. Reply “that's wrong” and it becomes a correction.

To your coding agents

Through MCP, an agent gets a governed answer with its receipt, never raw SQL.

As your own analysts
