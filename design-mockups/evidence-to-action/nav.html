Seven destinations, as EVIDENCE → ACTION v2.0 §3–§4 draws them

Every page and tab you can reach today has a place here: 22 nav rows, plus the tabs inside Briefing, Agent Ops, Record, Security, Evals, the Semantic Layer and Settings. Click any destination or section above; the page heading says where it lives today. Page bodies are unchanged on purpose. This mockup is about where things live, not what they look like.

What the doc decides, and what it leaves open

  • Top level: at most seven (§3). Notifications, help, settings and admin move to the top bar as global chrome.
  • The nav is 224px wide and collapses to 56px (§4.2), with icon and label. Below 1280px it collapses by default (§4.4). The collapse control is at the nav's foot.
  • The doc says nothing about a second level. Its own breadcrumb ("Analyze › SQL › …") shows one exists. Here a destination's sections open beneath it, one destination at a time, and clicking a destination opens its first section.
  • Labels match everywhere (§3): the route, page title, breadcrumb and ⌘K all use the section's name.
  • Unsupported destinations are hidden, not dead links. Notebooks aren't built, so Analyze doesn't list them.

What it costs

  • No page gets fewer clicks, and 31 of 55 get one more. Today all 22 rows are always on screen, so every row is one click. Here only a destination's first section is one click.
  • What softens it: a destination could reopen the section you last used there, and ⌘K reaches any page by name (it already reaches Playbook, which has no row). Neither changes the count for a page you haven't opened yet.
  • What it buys: room. The tallest destination open (Monitor, 8 sections) ends at 524px, where today's rows run to 830 of 852 at 1440×900. A new page no longer pushes another below the fold.
  • With the Pramanyx-safe names, most of it is today's grouping folded up. Operations is today's Operations plus Health and Brain map. The real moves are Briefing's ten tabs spreading across four destinations, and Inbox and Record joining under Decide.

Calls I made that need your yes

  • The doc has no destination for what Pramanyx knows (today's whole "Intelligence" group). I placed those pages by best fit: Profile → Data, Org and Memory → Model, Evidence → Decide, Brain map → Monitor. This is the biggest gap the exercise found.
  • Record stays whole under Decide. The doc would split it, with Inquiries under Ask and Decisions under Decide. You approved Record as one row with five views.
  • Agent Ops stays whole under Monitor (your rule: its tabs stay as they are).
  • Spreadsheets → Analyze rather than Data, since people work in sheets. Documents → Data, as material the agents read.
  • Briefing → Home, which the doc defines as "briefings, recent, assigned". Now also moves to Home, and Inbox to Decide.
  • Integrations → the Settings menu in the top bar, beside Organization, Access, Appearance, Models and System.
  • The Notifications page (what the platform sends) → Monitor. The top-bar bell is new: it's for what's sent to you, and nothing feeds it yet.
