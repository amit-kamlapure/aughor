Ontology — what it is for, and a page you can use

The ontology is the business's own model of itself, above the tables: the things it deals in (orders, customers, products), how they connect, the stages they go through, the promises it makes on them, the groups that matter, what may be done to them and the metrics that measure them. Agents, the Briefing, dashboards and Actions all speak it.

Two jobs, kept apart

  • Read the business — what do we have, how many, how are they doing, which promises are breaking — then act.
  • Keep the model right — confirm what the explorer proposed, fix a key, a mapping, a link; release.

What is wrong today

  • Three panes at once at 1440: a rail of five kinds of thing, a map no one can read, a type squeezed into a third.
  • What waits for you sits in four places (the bar's "6 to approve", the rail's draft, the type's Needs you, "4 messages").
  • Reading and governing are on screen together, all the time.

What changes (nothing is removed)

  • Home is a register of object types — one readable table (the study, §11.2) — with processes and rules under it.
  • A type is a page: Overview · Properties · Relationships · Mapping · Objects · Rules & sets · Actions · Impact · History.
  • Everything that waits is one queue, "Review". The map is a view you pick, one hop from a type.
