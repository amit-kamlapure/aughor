The Briefing, as EVIDENCE → ACTION v2.0 §13.3 draws it

The doc's rule is "a briefing is ranked evidence, not a KPI wall": the period and its freshness, a one-paragraph summary with linked claims, a ranked table of changes, a compact figure only where it adds something, then what waits on a person. It's drawn here from what theLook's Record holds, with no model call: no edition is written for theLook at any range, and writing one costs a model call.

What the doc changed here

  1. No lead figure. The page opens with the range's honesty (29 days held back), then a counted summary, then the ranked table. CLARITY would lead with one large figure; the doc has none above 15px.
  2. Every number says where it came from. The summary is labelled Computed. Each answer opens in the inspector with its run, its investigation, and the SQL it kept.
  3. Recorded against read again. Five answers kept their revenue rule (sale price, cancelled lines out). Read again today, four of them have changed, by up to −82%: theLook redrew those days after the runs read them. Only 1 Nov still holds. Today's Briefing re-reads its findings too when it opens (about 450 queries on theLook, measured 12 Oct); this draws the result as a column.
  4. The queue is one table: disputes, alerts, proposals, gaps and reviews, each with its next step on the row.

What it surfaced that today's Briefing doesn't say

  • The scheduled revenue answers for 26–29 Oct reported a ramp ($43.9K → $90.2K) that theLook has since redrawn to about $16–19K a day. All seven are still Provisional, which is right, but nothing on screen says four of them have moved.
  • The desk lists the same proposed investigation twice ("slow" and "slowed"); here they're folded into one row.
  • Two proposals carry raw error text in their stored titles ("404 Not found: Table project-f9f4…"). Here they read as plain words, with the stored text kept behind Details.
